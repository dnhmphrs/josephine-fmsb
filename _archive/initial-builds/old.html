On the practice

The work moves vertically — a question raised on a picket line might be carried into a regulator's consultation, then into a multilateral working group, then back down as policy that has to survive contact with the people it governs.

Most failures in tech policy are translation failures: a misread of who has leverage, which institution actually decides, what language a room can hear. The brief is to reduce that gap.

Based in Berlin and working across Europe and Asia. Fluent in English and Mandarin, with a Chinese background and a working understanding of how policy is made — and unmade — inside those systems.

Clients have included national governments, international organisations, labour federations, philanthropic funders, and a small number of technology firms working on questions of public interest.
